Elementary Tutoring in Montreal

Who this service is for

Elementary students who need help catching up, staying on track, preparing for a test, or feeling more comfortable with schoolwork.

Common challenges

  • Homework is becoming stressful
  • Reading, writing, or math foundations feel uneven
  • The student needs clearer explanations and more practice

How Shine Bright helps

  • Break lessons into manageable steps
  • Build skills through targeted practice
  • Encourage questions, confidence, and steady routines

Subjects and goals

  • Math
  • French
  • English
  • Science
  • Homework help
  • Multi-subject review

Frequently asked questions

Can sessions cover more than one subject?

Yes. Support can adapt to current homework, classroom priorities, and the student’s learning goals.

Is elementary tutoring available online?

Yes. Online sessions are available across Québec, with in-person tutoring in Montreal when available.

Can you help my child prepare for a test?

Yes. Sessions can focus on review, practice questions, study habits, and confidence before a test.
