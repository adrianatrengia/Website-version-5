Who this service is for

CEGEP students looking for help with course material, assignments, midterms, finals, or a stronger math foundation.

Common challenges

  • Lectures move too quickly
  • One missed concept affects the next chapter
  • Practice problems feel less clear than classroom examples

How Shine Bright helps

  • Work through problems step by step
  • Identify the concept behind common errors
  • Build an efficient review plan for evaluations

  • Final exam preparation

Frequently asked questions

Which CEGEP math courses are covered?

Current offerings include Calculus 1, Calculus 2, and Linear Algebra. Contact Shine Bright with the course name to confirm fit.

Can I book before a midterm or final?

Yes. Early booking is encouraged during busy exam periods.

Are online CEGEP sessions available?

Yes. Online tutoring is available across Québec and works well for guided problem-solving and review.
