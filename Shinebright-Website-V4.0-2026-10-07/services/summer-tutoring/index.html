Summer Tutoring in Montreal

Low-pressure summer learning that keeps important skills active, closes selected gaps, and prepares students for what comes next.

Book now →Ask a question →

Who this service is for

Students who want to maintain skills, revisit difficult concepts, prepare for a new level, or learn at a calmer pace.

Common challenges

  • Skills can fade during a long break
  • The previous year left a few gaps
  • The next level feels intimidating

How Shine Bright helps

  • Create a flexible goal-based plan
  • Mix review with engaging practice
  • Prepare foundations without recreating school-year pressure

Subjects and goals

  • Math foundations
  • French and English literacy
  • Science review
  • Preparation for the next grade
  • Entrance exam foundations

Frequently asked questions

Is summer tutoring intensive?

It can be light or focused. The plan is adapted to the student’s goals and family schedule.

Can we prepare for the next grade?

Yes. Sessions can reinforce prerequisites and introduce key foundations for the next level.

Is summer tutoring available online?

Yes. Online sessions are available across Québec.
