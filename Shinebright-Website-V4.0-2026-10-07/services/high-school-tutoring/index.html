Who this service is for

Secondary students who want weekly support, focused help in one subject, or a practical plan for midterms and finals.

Common challenges

  • A course has become hard to follow
  • Assignments and studying feel overwhelming
  • Marks or confidence have started to slip

How Shine Bright helps

  • Revisit core concepts in plain language
  • Create focused review and practice
  • Support planning, problem-solving, and independent learning

Subjects and goals

  • Math (CST, TS, and SN)
  • Science (ST and EST)
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • French
  • English

Frequently asked questions

Which math pathways do you support?

Support includes CST, TS, and SN. Contact Shine Bright to confirm the right tutor and current availability.

Can tutoring be short-term?

Yes. Tutoring can focus on one unit or exam, or continue weekly for steady support.

Do you help with science courses?

Yes. Support may include science, physics, and chemistry depending on level and tutor availability.
