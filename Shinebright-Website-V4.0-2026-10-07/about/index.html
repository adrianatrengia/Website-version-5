About Shine Bright and Adriana Trengia

Personalized academic support built on patience, clarity, and confidence.

Meet the founder

Adriana Trengia

Adriana is the founder of Shine Bright Tutoring MTL, a local educator and tutor dedicated to student progress.

She created Shine Bright to offer one-on-one support where students can ask questions, understand ideas at their pace, and build lasting skills. Her approach begins with each learner’s level, needs, and way of understanding.

For six years, the company has supported families in Montreal’s East End and online across Québec. The goal is to strengthen academic skills while helping students feel more capable and confident.
