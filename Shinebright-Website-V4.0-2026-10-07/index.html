Parent questions

Simple answers before you begin.

How do I book a session?

Use any Book Now button to open the official Setmore page, choose a service, and view current availability.

Do you offer online tutoring?

Yes. Online one-on-one tutoring is available across Québec.

What subjects do you cover?

Support includes French, English, math, science, core elementary and high school subjects, and specialized CEGEP math.

Do you help with entrance exams?

Yes. Preparation can include French, English, mathematical reasoning, reading, writing, vocabulary, logic, timed practice, and confidence strategies.

Do you support elementary and high school students?

Yes. Shine Bright supports elementary and high school learners, as well as CEGEP math students.

Can tutoring be short-term or ongoing?

Yes. Support can focus on one assignment or exam, or continue weekly depending on the student’s needs and availability.

Do you offer summer tutoring?

Yes. Summer sessions can maintain skills, close selected gaps, and prepare for the next school year.

How can I contact Shine Bright?

Call or text +1 438 398 7996, or email shinebrighttutoringmtl@gmail.com.
